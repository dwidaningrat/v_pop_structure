# Population Structure of the Viridans Group Streptococci: Pangenome and Phylogenetic Analyses

This repository contains the scripts and analytical workflows developed for my DPhil thesis investigating the population structure of viridans group streptococci (VGS) through comparative genomics, pangenome analysis, and phylogenetic approaches.

## Overview

The analyses in this repository were developed to process and analyse bacterial genomic data and to investigate genomic diversity and population structure within the VGS.

The repository includes scripts for:

- genome assembly and quality-control workflows;
- genome annotation;
- pangenome analysis and post-processing;
- phylogenetic reconstruction and comparison;
- sequence alignment and extraction;
- population structure and clustering analyses;
- analysis of capsular polysaccharide (CPS) regions; and
- processing and summarisation of genomic analysis outputs.

## Repository contents

The analytical workflow uses a combination of **Python**, **R**, and **shell scripts**, together with established bioinformatics tools.

Examples include:

- **PIRATE** for bacterial pangenome analysis;
- **Prokka** for genome annotation;
- **MAFFT** for multiple sequence alignment;
- **IQ-TREE** for phylogenetic inference;
- **ClonalFrame**-based analyses for investigating recombination and phylogenetic relationships;
- **BLAST**-based analyses for sequence comparison and identification; and
- custom Python and R scripts for data processing, statistical analysis, clustering, and summarisation.

## Reproducibility

Scripts have been retained to document the computational and analytical steps used, from processing genomic data through to downstream pangenome and phylogenetic analyses.

Many of the shell scripts act as wrappers for individual stages of the workflow, while Python and R scripts perform data processing, analysis, and summarisation of results.

## Research context

This work formed part of my DPhil research into the genomic diversity and population structure of viridans group streptococci.

## Research context

This work formed part of my DPhil research into the genomic diversity and population structure of VGS.
The analyses were designed to:
- describe the pangenome of VGS, encompassing evaluation of genome-based species classifications, characterisation of the VGS pangenome, evaluation of the shared genetic features among different species and groups within the VGS designation, characterisation of the shared genetic features into core and accessory, and description of the functional categories of the characterised gene families within the VGS pangenome.
- assess the population structure and species demarcation within VGS through a core genome phylogeny approach, encompassing the evaluation of the population structure of VGS inferred from the core-genome-based phylogenetic analysis; the comparison of the core-genome-based species delineation with genome-based species classification and species assignments reported in the metadata; the evaluation of species phylogenetic clustering using ANI; and the comparison of different phylogenetic approaches (single-gene, multi-gene, core-genome) and their recombination-adjusted phylogenetic analyses.
- evaluate the presence of pneumococcal cps-like loci in non-pneumococcal strains, encompassing the identification of pneumococcal cps-like loci in non-pneumococcal genomes through homology searches for regions resembling pneumococcal capsular genes, CPS biosynthetic components, and full-length operons responsible for the pneumococcal CPS biosynthesis pathway.
